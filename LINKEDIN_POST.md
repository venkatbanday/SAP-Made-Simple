I built **SAP Made Simple** — a free, interactive learning hub designed to make SAP easier to understand and practise.

Learning SAP shouldn't mean memorising transaction codes without understanding the business process behind them.

The updated app brings together:

• SAP modules and end-to-end process explanations
• Real-world troubleshooting case studies
• Transaction-code finder and study flashcards
• Practice quizzes and interview preparation
• BOM variance calculator and common error finder
• Six-week learning roadmap with progress tracking
• Personal study notes and progress export

It works directly in a web browser and is free to explore. No account required.
